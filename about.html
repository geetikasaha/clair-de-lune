The Reader of Chaos

Not a teacher. Not a guide. A mirror — held steady so you can finally see.

The Collapse That Opened Everything

My path into the unseen did not begin with a workshop or a retreat. It began with collapse — a decade-long unraveling of everything I thought I was. Losing what I loved. Losing the version of myself I had built so carefully. When the structures fall and silence is all that remains, you either disappear into it, or you learn to listen.

I chose to listen.

"The chaos was not my enemy. It was the first honest thing that had ever spoken to me."

The Awakening (2024 – 2025)

In 2024, something shifted in the silence. What I had been carrying for years — the weight, the grief, the searching — began to reorganize itself into something I could finally name. I started to see the invisible patterns in people's lives with startling clarity. Not as predictions. As truths already in motion, waiting to be acknowledged.

I began to sit with people in their darkest hours. And what I found, again and again, is that the answer was never missing. It was buried under noise. Under fear. Under the voice that says you are too broken to know your own way. That voice lies. I have watched people step into their exact destiny, simply because someone finally held space for them to hear themselves.

Two Fires: Krishna & Kaali

I carry two forces within me, and they are not opposites — they are partners.

Krishna is the knowing that holds you. Still. Playful. The one who says: everything is already perfect, even this. He is the part of me that sits with you in your pain without trying to fix it — because some things are not meant to be fixed. They are meant to be felt fully, so you can finally move through them.

Kaali is the truth that burns. Fierce. Uncompromising. The one who looks at what you are clinging to and says: that was never yours. She is the part of me that will not let you leave a reading carrying a comfortable lie. She loves you enough to be ruthless.

"In every reading, both are present. One to hold you. One to free you."

What I Believe

I do not believe the answers are outside of you. I believe they live in the chaos — in the feeling you keep ignoring, in the pattern that keeps repeating, in the moment right before you understand. The cards do not predict. They reveal what is already true in your energy, in this moment.

My work is not to give you a roadmap. It is to help you hear the knowing that has been speaking inside you all along — the one you have been too afraid, too busy, or too hurt to trust. When you leave a session with me, I want you to feel not that I told you something, but that you finally heard yourself.

The chaos is not your enemy. It is your signal. And I am here to help you read it.
